🔥 Ongoing Research Projects (v3.0 Development)

R.1: In Silico High-Throughput Screening for Novel Sirtuin Activators ▼

Utilizing the highly efficient STACPRED ensemble to perform virtual screening against commercial databases (ZINC, PubChem) containing over 1 million compounds. The goal is to identify novel, structurally diverse **Sirtuin activators** outside of the known scaffold class for experimental validation.

View Virtual Screening Methods
R.2: QM/MM Study of Sirtuin-1 Reaction Mechanism ▼

Applying **Quantum Mechanics/Molecular Mechanics (QM/MM)** hybrid simulations to model the bond-breaking and formation steps in the Sirtuin-1 deacetylation reaction. This deep computational study aims to identify key catalytic residues and the energy barriers involved, informing future drug design efforts targeting the enzyme mechanism directly.

Watch QM/MM Theory Talk
R.3: STACPRED v3 Development - Integrating GCN and Robust OOD Detection ▼

The next generation model focuses on two key upgrades: **Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs)** for superior feature extraction from molecular topology, and implementing a strict **Tanimoto Similarity** filter to ensure all predictions fall within the model's reliable domain (Out-of-Domain prevention).
