Meet the Developer

Malaka Sandaruwan

Principal Investigator / BPharm

This project is driven by my curiosity and interest in the endless possibilities at the intersection of Pharmacy and Machine Learning. As an undergraduate student of the Bachelor of Pharmacy program at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University, Sri Lanka, my goal is to contribute to the scientific community by developing transparent, high-accuracy tools. This work utilizes modern generative AI assistants (like Gemini and ChatGPT) for architectural insights and script refinement, blending domain knowledge with advanced computational methods.

Technical Contributions to STACPRED

  • Architectural Lead: Designed the foundational 8-model Stacking Ensemble to minimize prediction variance and maximize R² score.
  • Feature Engineering: Implemented the robust 2000+ feature pipeline, integrating 4 distinct docking results (AD4, Vina, etc.) with comprehensive ECFP4 and 3D structural metrics.
  • Model Optimization: Hand-tuned the hyperparameters for both the deep MLP (256, 128, 64, 32) and the high-capacity XGBoost (low-rate, max-depth 9) pipelines.
  • Deployment Ready: Implemented the prediction workflow logic for seamless, single-input, low-latency scoring for a potential RESTful API integration.

Open to collaborate and learn more! Connect with me to discuss future projects in computational pharmacology.
